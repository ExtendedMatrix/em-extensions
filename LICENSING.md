# Licences

| What | Where | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| The text of the documents | `*.md` | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| The index and its lists | `index.json`, `blocklist.json`, `reviewed.json`, `sources.lock.json` | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Tools and tests | `tools/`, `tests/`, `.github/` | [EUPL-1.2](https://interoperable-europe.ec.europa.eu/collection/eupl/eupl-text-eupl-12) |
| Plugins | `plugins/<id>/` | what each plugin declares in its envelope (`license`) |
| The archives the index names | served beside it | what each archive declares: the index copies its `license` and, for every vocabulary it carries, its licence and tier |

**Why CC0 for the index.** Anybody may mirror it, filter it, merge it with
another repository's, without a notice.

**An archive's own licence is its own.** The index repeats what an archive
says of itself and never relicenses anything.
